# Website direction

- Keep the painterly garden artwork and the familiar scrolling layout.
- Homepage: garden hero and immediately visible projects. About and Notes are separate pages; Mango belongs on About. Use a compact footer.
- Notes must stay empty until Varun supplies or approves writing. Never invent entries.
- VirtualPet and Hot Wheeler belong under Games.
- Use plain section names and specific descriptions. Default to one short sentence per project.
- Remove repeated introductions, decorative captions, taglines, and vague claims. Do not fill empty space with prose.
- Do not add phrases such as “ideas made tangible,” “always a work in progress,” or “following curiosities.”
- Projects should be understandable at a glance: real previews, short descriptions, and visible direct links. Do not hide the work behind accordion rows.
- Preserve the user's actual writing. Do not invent biography, achievements, metrics, or published articles.
- Keep interactions subtle, keyboard accessible, responsive, and compatible with reduced motion.
- When a working local preview is requested, leave its server running for review and provide its exact URL.
